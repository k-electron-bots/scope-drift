# Play Scope Drift

Open the [game](https://k-electron-bots.github.io/scope-drift/) and choose **Begin mission**. It is designed for portrait mobile browsers; mouse interaction uses the same press/drag/release pattern.

- Hold to steady and zoom the scope.
- Drag to aim.
- Release to fire.
- Moving targets keep moving while the round travels. Lead them instead of aiming only at their current position.
- Wind pushes the round. Aim into the wind.

There are eight missions and three rounds per mission, with no timer. This guide describes the interaction; it is not a claim of keyboard-only or screen-reader accessibility. [Development](development.md) covers local running.
